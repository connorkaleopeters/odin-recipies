Cheese Pizza Recipe

Home

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups of self-rising flour, plus more for kneading
  • 1 cup plain Greek yogurt
  • cooking spray
  • grated cheese
  • marinara sauce

Recipe

  1. Gather all ingredients. Preheat the oven to 500 degrees F (260 degrees C).
  2. Mix flour and Greek yogurt together in a large bowl to form a dough; transfer to a work surface floured with self-rising flour.
  3. Knead dough, adding more flour as needed to keep dough from being too sticky, 8 to 10 minutes.
  4. Coat a 12-inch pizza pan with cooking spray and spread dough to the edges of the pan.
  5. Bake crust in the preheated oven for 5 to 7 minutes; add toppings and return to the preheated oven.
  6. Bake until crust is lightly browned, 5 to 7 minutes more.
  7. Enjoy!
